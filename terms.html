Terms and Conditions

Updated 14 October 2026

These terms cover the Wanderlust World website, member accounts, and the sample flight bookings on this site.

Accounts

Creating an account stores your name, email, and a hash of your password in this browser. Wanderlust World does not send account details to a server. You can sign out at any time. If you clear this browser’s site data, the account and its saved trips are removed. Do not share a device if you want to keep the account private.

Sample bookings

Flight search and booking on this site are a demonstration. Fares, seats, and timetables are sample data. Confirming a booking does not take payment and does not create a reservation with an airline. A booking reference is generated on this device only.

Saved itineraries

When you continue as a member, a confirmed trip is saved to My Itinerary for the signed-in account on this device. Guest bookings stay on this device and are not added to a member itinerary. You can remove a saved trip from My Itinerary. Removing it deletes the local record. It does not cancel a ticket, because no ticket was issued.

Your responsibilities

Use the site for personal trip planning and this demonstration. Do not attempt to disrupt the site or access another person’s account on a shared device. Check the name and email you enter before confirming a sample booking.

Changes

These terms may be updated on this page. The date above shows the current version. If you keep using an account after an update, you accept the terms shown at that time.
